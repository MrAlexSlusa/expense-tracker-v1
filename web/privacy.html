Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 September 2026

Expense Tracker is a personal project run by one person, not a company. It has no analytics, no advertising, no trackers, and no third-party scripts. Nothing you enter is sold, shared for marketing, or used to profile you. This page describes exactly what it does hold and why.

Who runs this

Alexandru Slusarencu, an individual, operating this app as a personal project. For any question about your data, or to request deletion, email alex.slusarencu@gmail.com.

What is collected

Your account

  • Your email address, which identifies the account.
  • If you set a password, only a bcrypt hash of it. The password itself is never stored and cannot be recovered from the hash.
  • If you sign in with Google or GitHub, the provider name and the user ID that provider assigns you, so a later sign-in resolves to the same account.
  • An optional display name and profile picture. The picture is stored directly in the database as an embedded image, not on a separate image host.
  • Your currency preference and whether two-factor login is switched on.

What you record

  • Expenses — amount, category, any note you write, the date, and which account it came from.
  • Income lines — name and amount, per month.
  • Budget categories, their monthly targets, and your Wants/Needs/Savings split.
  • Accounts — the name and type you give them, the last four digits if you enter them, and a balance you maintain yourself. This app never connects to a bank, so it cannot see real balances or transactions.

If you use the WhatsApp bot

  • Your phone number, and the full original text of messages you send it. The raw text is kept alongside the parsed amount so a mis-parsed expense can be checked against what you actually wrote.

Sign-in codes

If two-factor login or a password reset is used, a six-digit code is emailed to you and stored hashed, with an expiry, and marked used once redeemed.

What is never collected

  • No banking credentials, card numbers, or payment details — there is nothing to pay for and no field that asks.
  • No location, contacts, device identifiers, or advertising IDs.
  • No behavioural analytics. The app loads no third-party JavaScript at all, so no outside party sees your visit.

Cookies and browser storage

This app sets no cookies. It signs you in with a token held in your browser's own storage:

  • Your sign-in token. With "Save my login info" on it goes in localStorage and survives closing the browser; with it off it goes in sessionStorage and is gone when the tab closes.
  • Your email address, to prefill the login form — only while "Save my login info" is on. Switching it off deletes it.
  • Your theme and language choices.

All of it stays in your browser and is readable only by this app. Logging out clears the token. Clearing site data clears the rest.

Who else touches your data

These are service providers doing a specific job, not partners receiving data to use for their own ends:

  • Render — hosts the server and the database where your data lives.
  • GitHub Pages — serves the app's static files. It receives ordinary web-server request data, not your account contents.
  • Google or GitHub — only if you choose to sign in with them. They confirm your verified email address and basic profile to this app. This app requests nothing else: it cannot read your Google Drive, Gmail, or your GitHub repositories.
  • Resend — only to deliver two-factor and password-reset emails, and only if email sending is configured.
  • Twilio — only if you use the WhatsApp bot, since your messages reach the app through them.
  • Google Sheets — only if the optional spreadsheet mirror is switched on, in which case expense amounts and categories are written into a spreadsheet you own.

Your data is not sold, rented, or given to anyone else.

How long it is kept

Your data stays until you remove it. Individual expenses, income lines, categories and accounts can be deleted inside the app at any time.

You can delete your whole account from inside the app — Accounts → Delete account. It erases everything belonging to it: every expense, category, account, income line and sign-in code, plus the account itself. Nothing is kept behind a flag, it cannot be undone, and it takes effect immediately. If you would rather ask, email alex.slusarencu@gmail.com.

Security

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt, sign-in tokens are signed and expire, and all traffic runs over HTTPS. Social sign-ins are matched only on an email address the provider states it has verified, so someone cannot reach your account by registering your address elsewhere.

Being straightforward about it: this is a personal project maintained by one person, not an audited service with a security team. It is built with care, but do not treat it as a system of record for anything you cannot afford to lose.

Your choices

  • See your data — all of it is visible in the app; ask by email for a copy in a machine-readable form.
  • Correct it — every field is editable in the app.
  • Delete it — item by item in the app, or the whole account at once from Accounts → Delete account.
  • Withdraw a sign-in method — revoke this app's access in your Google account or GitHub settings at any time. Your data here is unaffected; you would sign in another way.

Children

This app is not intended for children under 16 and is not directed at them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will be noted in the app.
